 Gearbox Fault L663 Defender 110 D300 
 Click image to enlarge 
On Saturday morning my 2022 Defender 110 D300 came up with a gearbox fault whilst driving down a country lane. When I stopped the gearbox wouldn’t shift into park and displayed a fault on the dash and the gear shifter lights were not illuminating. 
It wouldn’t shift into drive or reverse and I tried locking it and unlocking etc. 
I checked all of the relevant fuses, which all looked ok and the fault remained.
I called LR assist and the AA technician was unable to clear the fault code, so the vehicle was recovered to the Norwich LR Dealership. 
3 days later LR assist are still sorting out a loan vehicle. Last edited by NewDefenderUK on 25th Jul 2025 9:37am. Edited 1 time in total
